The Premise

The Abandoned is a small, multi-level dungeon-crawler in which your simple task is to venture from room to room, either avoiding or mowing down any and all enemies in your path with your might sword. You'll occasionally come across healing potions as well to bring your health back up, in case you end up in a bit of a rough spot. So long as you don't lose by having your health reduced to zero through all the nasty little spirits that linger in the crypts, you'll reach the end of the dungeon and win!

The Purpose

This game was created the as the final project in my Secondary 4 Computer Science course in high-school back in 2022, where we were tasked with making a game using the game engine we had been learning about during the later half of the year: Godot. Similarly to my later-down-the-line game Peaks, I had complete control over the kind of game I wanted to create. At the time however I was engrossed in the then-popular mobile game Soul Knight, and so I chose to make a simple dungeon-crawler just like it.

Additional Notes

All visual assets regarding character and environmental sprites within The Abandoned where digital drawn and created by me, using Clip Studio Paint!
