The Geo Pile

*Visit the 'The Geo Pile' repository.*

The Premise

The Geo Pile is a non-function website for a fictional banking company set in the world of Hallownest, a vast kingdom ruled by pale gods and populated by massive populations of athropod-like folk.

The Purpose

This website was created as an assignment for my User Interfaces class during my second semester of college in 2024. We were tasked with creating a basic land-page of a non-functional website as though we were an up and coming new company. As I was at the time (and still am) infatuated with the game Hollow Knight, I decided to model my site around the game's world.

Additional Notes

The logo and employee character designs within The Geo Pile were illustrated by me. The characters however depicted in the home page, as well as the section separators, background images, and primary fonts, can all be credited to originating from Team's Cherry 2017 metroidvania game Hollow Knight.
